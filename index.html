HOW TO PLAY

Touch Drag on the left to steer and drive. Hold BRAKE to reverse, or with the gas on in a turn to drift. BOOST for nitro, FIRE for pickups.

Keys Arrows or WASD, Space boost, X fire, P pause, Tab names.

Scrapped? Tap (or Tab) to watch the next car, ZOOM (or Z) to pull back.

Score Smash scrap bots for points and boost. Crates give rockets, mines, repairs.

NEW HERE?
