HOW TO PLAY

Touch: drag on the left to steer and drive. Hold BRAKE to reverse or slide, BOOST for nitro, FIRE for pickups.
Keys: arrows/WASD, Space boost, X fire, P pause, Tab names.
Scrapped? Tap (or Tab) to watch the next car, ZOOM (or Z) to pull back.
Smash scrap bots for points and boost. Crates give rockets, mines, repairs.
